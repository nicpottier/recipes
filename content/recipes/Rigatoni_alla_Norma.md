Classic Sicilian pasta dish with eggplant, tomatoes, and ricotta.

# Ingredients
* 1 lb rigatoni pasta
* 2 medium eggplants, diced into 1-inch cubes
* 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/2 cup olive oil (divided)
* 1 medium onion, diced
* 1/2 cup ricotta salata cheese, grated (or substitute with ricotta cheese)
* 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Red pepper flakes (optional)

# Instructions
1. Salt the diced eggplant generously and let sit in a colander for 30 minutes to draw out moisture. Pat dry with paper towels.

2. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the eggplant cubes until golden brown on all sides, about 8-10 minutes. Remove and set aside.

3. In the same skillet, add remaining olive oil. Sauté the onion until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook for another minute.

4. Add crushed tomatoes, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes if using. Simmer for 15 minutes until the sauce thickens.

5. Meanwhile, cook rigatoni pasta according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.

6. Add the cooked eggplant back to the tomato sauce and simmer for 5 minutes to combine flavors.

7. Add the drained pasta to the sauce, tossing to coat. Add a splash of pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.

8. Remove from heat and stir in half of the ricotta salata and torn basil leaves.

9. Serve immediately, topped with remaining cheese and fresh basil.
